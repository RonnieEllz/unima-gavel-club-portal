# Quick Start Guide

This is the fastest way to use the portal.

## 1. Sign up

1. Open the site.
2. Click Join.
3. Complete the registration form.
4. Submit your details.
5. Log in with your email and password.

## 2. Log in

1. Open the Login page.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. You are taken to your dashboard.

## 3. Update your profile

1. Go to Dashboard.
2. Open Profile.
3. Update your details.
4. Save changes.

## 4. Check in to a meeting

1. Open the dashboard or meeting page.
2. Find an active meeting.
3. Check in before the meeting closes.
4. Confirm your attendance.

## 5. Admin approval

If your account is new, an admin may need to approve it before full access is active.

## 6. Admin tasks

Admins can:

- approve members
- create meetings
- open and close attendance
- publish updates and stories
- upload photos
- generate reports
- assign admin roles

## 7. Support

If you cannot log in, cannot see the dashboard, or cannot check in, contact the club administrator.
